# kallini_repro — Reproduction of Kallini et al. (2024) Experiment 1

Target: "Mission: Impossible Language Models" (ACL 2024 Best Paper;
arXiv:2401.06416) — the Stanford paper our paper builds on. This directory
reproduces their **Experiment 1** (test-set perplexities across the
impossibility continuum) for the **\*Shuffle and \*Reverse classes**
(9 languages), using THEIR dataset-creation code verbatim and a faithful
reimplementation of their training/evaluation for a single RTX 3080.

## What is verbatim

- Impossible-language datasets: generated by **their** `data/perturb.py` +
  `utils.py` (cloned from jkallini/mission-impossible-language-models),
  byte-identical perturbation functions, tokenizers, and filters.
- Their random seeds `[0, 14, 41, 53, 96]` (default: first 3 via `SEEDS`).
- Their packing: numpy rng(seed) shuffle of sentence-token lines, EOS-join,
  1024-token chunking (their `training/babylm_dataset.py`).
- Their eval: per-sentence perplexity on 10,000 perturbed test sentences
  (their `get_perplexities` is copied verbatim, MIT), geometric mean per
  checkpoint, checkpoints every 100 steps.
- GPT-2 small from scratch, seq 1024, warmup 300 → LR 6e-4 (their Appendix B),
  stability flags `reorder_and_upcast_attn`/`scale_attn_by_inverse_layer_idx`.

## Documented deviations (single-3080 reality)

1. Effective batch 512 → **128** (VRAM): tokens/run 1.57B → 0.39B (~3 epochs).
2. LR: warmup 300 → 6e-4, then **linear decay to 0** by step 3000 (paper
   specifies only the warmup; their 4000-warmup note implies decay).
3. Eval checkpoints subset {100, 300, 500, 1000, 2000, 3000} of their
   100-step ladder; evaluated in-process (no intermediate disk checkpoints).
4. \*Hop languages (word_hop/token_hop/no_hop) are OUT OF SCOPE this round:
   their pipeline requires Stanza POS/morph tagging (GPU-hours) and their
   Experiment-1 perplexities showed minimal differences within that class
   anyway. Their Experiments 2-3 (surprisal, causal abstraction) not attempted.
5. BabyLM corpus from HF mirror `Sree1994/babylm_100M` (single concatenated
   genre file instead of their 10 genre files); sentence segmentation via
   regex shim (`shim_tag.py`) instead of Stanza (Shuffle/Reverse
   perturbations do not consume POS annotations; hop would).

## Files

| file | role |
|---|---|
| `shim_tag.py` | stanza-free `*_parsed.json` writer in their exact format |
| `train_exp1.py` | one (language, seed) run: pack → train → checkpoint evals |
| `kallini_queue.sh` | idempotent data-prep + training queue (this is what runs) |
| `aggregate_exp1.py` | exp1_summary.csv/md over (language × checkpoint) |
| `results/` | per-run JSON + per-checkpoint ppl tensors + summaries |

## Expected outputs (paper's Figure 2 narrative)

- `shuffle_control` (English) lowest ppl;
- `shuffle_nondeterministic` highest; deterministic shuffles between;
- `shuffle_local{3,10}` intermediate (smaller window better), `even_odd` close to control;
- `reverse_*` close to `reverse_control`, partial slightly better than full.

## Run

```bash
bash experiments_v2/kallini_repro/kallini_queue.sh          # SEEDS="0 14 41"
```
